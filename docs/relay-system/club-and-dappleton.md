## Orienting the club opening

These first-response meanings are context from P0251–P0257, not an unrestricted
opening-response trainer. The exercises start at the fixed prefixes below.

| Response to 1C | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1D | Four-plus hearts; preferred with 4/4 majors |
| 1H | Four-plus spades |
| 1S | (7)8+, usually no four-card major; excludes the 10+ single-suited six-plus diamond response |
| 1NT | 0–6(7), usually no good four-card major |
| 2C | 10+, six-plus diamonds, single-suited; **source only** |

Do not apply the natural-response [rebid reordering](reordered-rebids.md) to
the artificial 1C–1S route.

## 1C–1S: seven club exercises

South asks; North describes opener. Parenthesized source boundaries are
narrowed by each exercise's public practice constraints.

| Opener's rebid after 1C–1S | Enabled route | Practice subset |
|---|---|---|
| 1NT | 2S Baron, then 4D ordinary club Kickback | 14–15 no shortage, or 14–16 diamond shortage |
| 2C | 2S Baron, then 4D ordinary club Kickback | 16–19 no shortage, or 17–19 unbalanced; separate 2D resolution below |
| 2H | 3C club SKC or 3D diamond SKC | 14–16, spade shortage, exactly three diamonds |
| 2S | 3C club SKC or 3D diamond SKC | 14–16, heart shortage, exactly three diamonds |
| 2NT | 3C agrees clubs; 3D agrees diamonds; high/low shortage | 14–16, five/six clubs, exactly four diamonds |
| 3D | 3H agrees clubs; 3S agrees diamonds; high/low shortage | 9–13, five/six clubs, exactly four diamonds |

The seventh exercise is the strong 2C–2D resolution. The direct 3C/3H/3S
source rebids are not separate selectable exercises. Two-minor shortage
replies are high spades, then low hearts. Each completed shortage description
goes to SKC with the selected minor agreed.

### Baron answers to South's 2S

| North's reply | Meaning in the enabled subset |
|---|---|
| 2NT | No shortage, four-plus clubs |
| 3C | No shortage, four-plus diamonds |
| 3D | No shortage, neither four-card minor |
| 3H | Strong 2C-prefix only: 17–19, heart shortage |
| 3S | Strong 2C-prefix only: 17–19, spade shortage |
| 3NT | Diamond shortage; 14–16 after 1NT, 17–19 after 2C |

This exercise gives South six clubs/four diamonds, 12–20, and follows the
explicit 4D club-Kickback continuation. It excludes ambiguous double-four-minor
and special long-minor balanced responses. Ordinary Kickback does not add a
void rung even when the Baron reply has shown shortage. The source's later
swaps of describing hand are not all implemented.

### Strong resolution: 1C–1S–2C–2D

The user confirmed **balanced always 3NT, including 19/20**, and **16+ for
unbalanced descriptions**. This overrides the older balanced 2NT exception.
Practice caps are 20 balanced and 22 unbalanced; South has both minors and no
four-card major. Sources: P0420–P0436 plus that clarification.

| North's reply | Description in the admitted shape set | South's next source action |
|---|---|---|
| 2H | Spade shortage, five-plus clubs, two/three diamonds | 3C club SKC; or 3D with five diamonds |
| 2S | Heart shortage, five-plus clubs, two/three diamonds | 3C club SKC; or 3D with five diamonds |
| 3C | Long clubs, diamond shortage | 3D shortage enquiry, clubs agreed |
| 3D | Five-plus clubs/four-plus diamonds | 3H agrees clubs or 3S agrees diamonds; high/low shortage |
| 3H | Six-plus clubs/five hearts | 4C agrees clubs or 4D agrees hearts; high/low shortage |
| 3S | Six-plus clubs/five spades | 4C agrees clubs or 4D agrees spades; high/low shortage |
| 3NT | No shortage | To play, or choose a final contract |

The long-club exercise uses a single-shortage subset, which makes its remaining
3C hands diamond-short. That deduction is a practice restriction, not a new
general opening definition. The two-minor description is relocated to 3D;
there is no old 2NT two-minor answer after this resolution.

| After strong 2M, South 3D with five diamonds | North's answer |
|---|---|
| Exactly two diamonds | 3NT to play |
| Three diamonds, known shortage is void | 3H |
| Three diamonds, singleton shortage and 0/3 KC | 3S |
| Three diamonds, singleton shortage and 1/4 KC | 4C |
| Three diamonds, singleton shortage and 2/5 without Q | 4D |
| Three diamonds, singleton shortage and 2/5 with Q | 4H |

Here **3NT is reserved for the doubleton reply** and is excluded from the
three-diamond SKC answer ladder. This is an explicit exception to ordinary
consecutive response counting. Direct 2M exercises restrict opener to three
diamonds; the doubleton branch is exercised through the strong resolution.

## Dappleton: who is asking

You are **North, opener**. South's hidden hand describes responder. The enabled
subset uses North's 18/19 no-shortage 1NT rebid, with two/three in the transferred
major; South is 12–19. The source also covers other balanced ranges, but the
exceptional four-card-major escapes are excluded here.

| Major | Opening transfer prefix |
|---|---|
| Hearts | 1C–1D–1NT |
| Spades | 1C–1H–1NT |

| Responder route after that prefix | Cards promised in the exercises | North's next action |
|---|---|---|
| Direct 2NT | Four major/five clubs | 3C accepts clubs and asks shortage |
| Direct 3C | Four major/five diamonds | 3D accepts diamonds and asks shortage |
| 2C–2D puppet, then 2NT | Five major/five clubs | 3C asks shortage in clubs, or 3M agrees major and asks shortage |
| 2C–2D puppet, then 3C | Five major/five diamonds | 3D asks shortage in diamonds, or 3M agrees major and asks shortage |

The optional five-major agreement requires North to have exactly three in the
major and exactly three in the shown minor; “good” major support remains a
player judgement. All transfer routes exclude a second four-card major.

| Hidden South's known major/minor | Shortage answer alphabet | Outside suits to compare |
|---|---|---|
| Four major/five minor | No shortage, high shortage, low shortage | Delete the two known suits; rank the other two high/low |
| Five major/five minor | High shortage, low shortage | No-shortage answer is impossible |

Examples of the complete minor-acceptance ladders:

| Route through North's ask | South's replies |
|---|---|
| Hearts, four/five clubs, North 3C | 3D no shortage; 3H spade shortage; 3S diamond shortage |
| Spades, four/five clubs, North 3C | 3D no shortage; 3H heart shortage; 3S diamond shortage |
| Hearts, four/five diamonds, North 3D | 3H no shortage; 3S spade shortage; 3NT club shortage |
| Spades, four/five diamonds, North 3D | 3H no shortage; 3S heart shortage; 3NT club shortage |
| Hearts, five/five clubs, North 3C | 3D spade shortage; 3H diamond shortage |
| Spades, five/five clubs, North 3C | 3D heart shortage; 3H diamond shortage |
| Hearts, five/five diamonds, North 3D | 3H spade shortage; 3S club shortage |
| Spades, five/five diamonds, North 3D | 3H heart shortage; 3S club shortage |

After North agrees a minor, later asks skip 3NT. After North agrees the major,
3NT can be a relay ask and game in that major is reserved. Answers do not
inherit those asking omissions. Sources: P0446–P0467, P0518–P0520.

## Dappleton Baron: four fixed entries

Each major has a club-fit and diamond-fit entry. The complete prefix is:

`1C–transfer–1NT–2C–2D–2S–Baron answer–minor agreement`.

The practice hand has exactly five major/four minor; North has exactly four
in that minor. This is separate from South's exactly-five-minor transfer route.

| North's Baron answer | South's fixed agreement | North asks about South | Alternative major agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2NT, four-plus clubs | 3C | 3D shortage ask, clubs agreed | 3H shortage ask, major agreed |
| 3C, four-plus diamonds | 3D | 3H shortage ask, diamonds agreed | 3S shortage ask, major agreed |

The alternative requires three cards in South's major in North's hand.
South answers no/high/low shortage in the two outside suits, then North
continues KC/SKC. Baron and transfer descriptions therefore generate **12
Dappleton exercises** in total: eight transfers plus four Baron entries.
